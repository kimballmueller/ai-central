02 — Month One

What ships in the first four weeks.

Nothing here waits on an onboarding period. Week one starts the day you say go.

Week 1

Audit & architecture

Days 1–7
Deliverable: audit doc + sitemap, in writing

Full technical audit of the current site

Crawl, index coverage, page speed and Core Web Vitals, broken links (including the niche buttons at the bottom that go nowhere), mobile rendering, and everything GoDaddy is breaking each time a page is touched.

Keyword & query research across all seven verticals

What a VP of Operations or a CFO actually types — by vertical and by market. Search volumes, difficulty, and intent, separated into "hiring a recruiter" queries and "looking for a job" queries so we stop attracting the second group.

Competitor analysis — 3–5 recruiting firms per niche

What they rank for, what pages they have that you don't, and where the gaps are. HOA and QSR first, since that's where the money and the momentum are.

AI-answer baseline

We run 20 prompts across ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews — "who recruits GMs for QSR franchises in Texas," and nineteen more like it — and record exactly where Sublime appears today. That's the scoreboard we measure against every month after.

Site architecture & messaging locked

Full sitemap, URL structure, and the positioning that goes on every page: 15% against an industry 20–25%, the 120-day warranty, and thirty years of operations behind the screen. You approve it once, then we build.

You get: the written audit, the keyword map, the competitor breakdown, and the sitemap — all of it yours to keep regardless of what happens next.

Home, About, For Clients, For Candidates, Contact. Modern, fast, and clean enough that an $8M company doesn't hesitate — written by us, approved by you.

Week 4

Launch & foundation

Days 22–30
Deliverable: live site, off GoDaddy, fully instrumented

Migration off GoDaddy

New hosting in your name, DNS cutover, and a full 301 redirect map so nothing you've earned gets dropped. No downtime, no lost email, no more tickets about pages disappearing when you touch something.

Technical SEO shipped

Title tags and meta descriptions on every page, header structure, internal linking, XML sitemap and robots.txt, image compression and speed optimization, mobile and Core Web Vitals passing, SSL and canonical tags clean.

Schema markup for AI answers

Organization, Service, LocalBusiness and FAQ schema across the site. This is a large part of how a language model knows what Sublime is and feels safe naming you — it's the difference between being on the internet and being legible to it.

Google Business Profile optimized

Categories, services, service areas, description, photos, and the Q&A section seeded with the questions buyers actually ask. Profile linked properly to the new site.

Measurement turned on

Google Analytics 4, Search Console, and form and call tracking — all in your accounts. From here on, every claim in the monthly report is checkable by you without asking me.

Straight answer on timing: the site is live in week four. Rankings are not. Google needs to re-crawl and re-index a rebuilt site, which takes 30–60 days before movement is even measurable. The site earns its keep as a closer long before it earns its keep as a lead source.

04 — How You'll Know It's Working

Three scoreboards, and an honest timeline.

SEO is the one line item where the honest answer is "not this month." Here's what to watch, and when each one should actually move.

Days 30–60

Leading indicators

Pages indexed, impressions in Search Console, site speed, crawl errors gone. These move first and they tell you the foundation took. If these don't move, something is wrong and I'll say so.

Days 60–120

Rankings & AI answers

Movement across the ~40 tracked terms, and Sublime showing up in more of the 20 tracked AI prompts. You're already #1 for premium HOA recruiting in Texas — this is about the other six verticals and the other markets.

Month 4+

Inbound conversations

Form fills and calls from decision makers, tracked and attributed. This is the number that matters and it's also the slowest — anyone who promises it in month two is selling you something.

The fastest return on this tier isn't a ranking.

It's what happens when a VP accepts a connection request, gets curious, and looks you up. That value lands the week the site goes live — every outreach conversation from that point forward is happening against a site that supports you instead of one that undercuts you. Rankings are the compounding part; the credibility is immediate.
