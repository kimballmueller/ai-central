⚠ And we found something in your tracker

Your v50 helper column reads 54mg (27mg+27mg) as 108 milligrams. It adds the stated total and the two components. The cell literally reads =108*E4995.

$129.99 ÷ 54 mg  =  $2.4072 / mg  ← actual
$129.99 ÷ 108 mg =  $1.2036 / mg  ← what your dashboard shows

Which means Peptidology looks like your cheapest source for Selank+Semax. It isn't — Grey Axis Research is, at $1.7995. Your index has been pointing at the wrong vendor. Four rows do it, all Peptidology, all exactly doubled.
