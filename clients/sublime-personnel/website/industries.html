How they connect

Most searches cross two of these.

A restaurant group needs a controller. A property management company needs an association accountant and a maintenance director. A commercial contractor needs a safety director and a risk advisor, and their insurance agency needs a producer who understands contractors.

That overlap is the practical argument for a multi‑vertical firm. When you have hired us once, the second search rarely starts from zero — we already know your business, your comp philosophy, and the kind of person who lasts there.
