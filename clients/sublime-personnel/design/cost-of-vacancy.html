How we calculate this

Daily cost = (salary × multiplier) ÷ 260 working days. Cost so far = daily cost × days open. Lost revenue this quarter = daily cost × 90 days. Our fee is 15%, 20% or 25% of first‑year compensation depending on the replacement guarantee you want behind the placement.

This is a planning estimate, not a quote. It deliberately ignores overtime, the cost of the work not getting done, and manager time spent covering — so if anything it reads low. It also ignores the largest compounding risk: an open seat absorbed by one person who then leaves as well, which costs you the training already invested, a second search, and the team’s rhythm on top. Your actual fee is agreed in writing before any search begins.

Why we publish this

Why we publish our fees.

Look at the largest hospitality, insurance and construction recruiters. Not one publishes a fee. You are expected to sit through a sales conversation before anyone will name a figure.

We take the opposite view. Tell us what you are able to invest in hiring across a year and we will tell you whether we can work within it. Sometimes the answer is no — which is a five-minute conversation rather than a wasted quarter.
