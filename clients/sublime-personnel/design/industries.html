In detail

What each
practice covers.

Every practice page carries the roles we place, how we screen for them, and the questions employers in that industry actually ask.

01

HOA & Property Management

In community association management, governance is everything. A manager who cannot run a board meeting, read the governing documents and hold a difficult board together is a liability, however well the résumé reads.

Roles we place: Community Association Manager, Portfolio Manager, On-Site / High-Rise General Manager, Director of Community Management, Regional Director, Assistant Community Manager.

02

Hospitality & Restaurant

There is a difference between a résumé that reads like a general manager and a person who can hold a building together on a Saturday night. Thirty years of restaurant operations sit behind every search we run in this practice.

Roles we place: General Manager, Multi-Unit / District Manager, Director of Operations, Executive Chef, Food & Beverage Director, Regional Vice President.

03

Insurance Sales, Account Management & Leadership

Insurance is a relationship business with a technical floor. We screen for both — the book and the book of knowledge — because agencies that hire on charm alone spend the following year cleaning up the file.

Roles we place: Commercial Lines Producer, Commercial Lines Account Manager, Personal Lines Account Manager, Account Executive, Underwriter, Claims Adjuster / Examiner.

04

Healthcare

We recruit healthcare professionals who strengthen patient care, improve operational performance and support organisational growth. Our focus spans clinics, specialty practices, physician groups, healthcare companies and other patient‑focused organisations.

Roles we place: Physicians and Medical Directors, Nurse Practitioners and Physician Assistants, Clinical and Practice Leadership, Practice and Clinic Managers, Healthcare Operations Leadership, Revenue Cycle and Financial Leadership.

05

Accounting & Finance

Accounting and finance are essential to every organisation and connect all the industries we serve. From growing businesses and professional service firms to associations, agencies, contractors, healthcare organisations and energy companies, we recruit professionals who bring accuracy, accountability and clarity to the numbers.

Roles we place: Controller, Assistant Controller, Staff & Senior Accountant, Accounting Manager, AP / AR Manager, FP&A Analyst.

06

Construction Management and Operations

Construction hiring is judged in the field, not the interview. We screen on project type, delivery method and dollar value, because a twelve‑million‑dollar tilt‑wall superintendent and a ninety‑million‑dollar healthcare superintendent are not interchangeable.

Roles we place: Project Manager, Senior Project Manager, Superintendent, Estimator, Preconstruction Manager, Project Executive.

07

QSR & Franchise

Franchise operations is its own discipline. Running units you own and coaching units somebody else owns require two different personalities, and hiring the wrong one produces a year of quiet friction.

Roles we place: Area Coach / Area Supervisor, Franchise Business Consultant, Director of Operations, Multi-Unit Manager, Training & Development Manager, New Store Opening Manager.

08

Energy, Oil & Gas

The energy market does not lack résumés. It lacks a way to tell, in a downturn or a build‑out, which of them belongs in your seat — and whether the person who thrived at a supermajor can operate without that budget behind them.

Roles we place: Drilling Engineer, Completions Engineer, Production Superintendent, HSE Manager, Turnaround Manager, Project Controls Lead.
