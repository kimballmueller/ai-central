Why us for this

Patient care and
the P&L, in one hire.

Healthcare organisations need experienced professionals who can balance quality patient care with operational and financial performance. We recruit clinical providers, practice leaders, operations professionals and executives who strengthen teams, improve efficiency, support growth and help healthcare organisations deliver better outcomes.

That balance is the whole difficulty of the hire. A clinician who cannot read a P&L and an administrator who cannot hold the confidence of the clinical staff fail in the same job for opposite reasons, and both look strong on paper. We screen for the side a candidate is weaker on, not the side they lead with.

What we screen for

What separates a résumé from a hire.

01

Licensure and credentialing

State licence, board certification, DEA where it applies, and standing — confirmed before presentation. Credentialing timelines are the most common reason a healthcare start date slips.

02

Setting and scale

A single specialty clinic, a twelve‑provider group and a multi‑site platform are different jobs with the same title. We map provider count, sites and who actually reported to them.

03

Revenue cycle literacy

Payer mix, denials, days in AR, and whether they have owned those numbers or only seen them. This is where operations candidates separate quickly.

04

Standing with clinical staff

Whether providers would follow them. We reference this with clinicians who worked under them, not only with the executives who hired them.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do you place physicians and advanced practice providers?

Yes — physicians, medical directors, nurse practitioners and physician assistants, alongside the practice and operations leadership around them. We confirm licence and board standing before anyone reaches your calendar.

Can you support a practice opening a new location?

Yes. New sites need clinical and administrative leadership hired against a build schedule, and credentialing has to start well before the doors open. Give us the opening date and we will work backwards from it.

How do your fees work?

A percentage of first‑year compensation, structured to the level of the role and the volume of work, and agreed in writing before the search begins.

How long does a search usually take?

For most roles the first slate reaches you in under 10 days from the briefing. We then take your feedback and come back with a tighter four. Time to offer depends more on your interview process than on ours.
