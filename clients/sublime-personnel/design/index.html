Method

Define.
Map.
Deliver.

The same three stages on every engagement, from a senior accountant to a regional vice president. What changes is the depth of the market map.

01

Define

A proper briefing: what the role genuinely requires, who it reports to, the compensation you can defend, and the conditions that are quietly non‑negotiable. If the brief cannot be filled as written, you hear it here rather than in month three.

02

Map

We identify who holds the role today across comparable organisations, who is credentialed, and who is quietly open to a conversation. The majority of our placements were not looking when we called.

03

Deliver

A short slate with written assessment of each candidate: the fit, the risk, the motivation, and what it will take to close them. We manage the process through to offer, including the counter‑offer.

FAQ

Before you call.

What industries do you recruit for?

Hospitality and restaurant, HOA and property management, commercial and personal lines insurance, accounting and finance, commercial construction, and QSR and franchise operations. Each practice is led by a partner with direct operating experience in it.

Where do you recruit?

We are based in the Greater Houston Area and recruit nationwide, with the deepest reach in Texas and the Gulf Coast.

How do your fees work?

A percentage of first-year compensation, structured to the level of the role and the volume of work, agreed in writing before the search begins. Tell us the budget you are working to and we will tell you whether we can operate within it.

Do you offer a guarantee?

Yes. Every direct hire carries a replacement guarantee of 60, 90 or 120 days — the term is negotiated and set in your contract. Should the placement leave inside that window, we run the search again at no further fee. We have written about why replacement guarantees matter here.

Is there any cost for candidates?

Never. Candidates are never charged. Our fees are paid by the hiring company.
