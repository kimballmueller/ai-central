Search & Website Services

Client:    Sublime Personnel (Pete & Terry)
Provider:  Disruptors Media - Kimball Mueller (kimball@disruptorsmedia.com)
Fee:       $850.00 per month. No setup fee. Month-to-month.
Date:      August 11, 2026


1. OVERVIEW

Provider will design, build, and host a new website for Client, migrate it off
GoDaddy, and perform ongoing search engine optimization (SEO), generative engine
optimization (GEO), and answer engine optimization (AEO) services.

This Scope of Work covers Search & Website services only. LinkedIn outreach is a
separate service offered at $850 per month; both services combined are $1,500 per
month.


2. MONTH ONE DELIVERABLES

2.1 Week One - Audit & Architecture
  a) Full technical audit of the existing website: crawl, index coverage, page
     speed and Core Web Vitals, broken links, mobile rendering, and hosting
     limitations.
  b) Keyword and search query research across all seven of Client's verticals,
     segmented by buyer intent (companies seeking recruiting services) versus
     candidate intent (individuals seeking employment).
  c) Competitor analysis covering three to five competing recruiting firms per
     niche, including ranking gaps and content gaps.
  d) AI answer baseline: twenty defined prompts run across ChatGPT, Perplexity,
     and Google AI Overviews, with Client's current visibility recorded. This
     baseline is the benchmark for ongoing monthly reporting.
  e) Site architecture, sitemap, URL structure, and positioning/messaging
     document, submitted to Client for written approval.

  Deliverables: written audit, keyword map, competitor analysis, sitemap,
  messaging document. All become Client property upon delivery.

2.2 Weeks Two and Three - Build
  a) Core pages designed, written, and built: Home, About, For Clients, For
     Candidates, Contact.
  b) Seven vertical landing pages, each with dedicated content, proof, and
     search targeting: hospitality/restaurant; HOA and property management;
     commercial lines insurance; personal lines insurance; accounting;
     commercial construction; QSR/franchise.
  c) Two written case studies developed from Client-supplied engagement history.
  d) Conversion paths: contact forms with email and SMS notification, calendar
     booking integration, and click-to-call on mobile, with separate paths for
     client inquiries and candidate inquiries.
  e) Google reviews, video, and existing social content integrated into
     the site.

  Deliverables: complete website on a private staging link for Client review
  prior to launch. One full revision round included on every page.

2.3 Week Four - Launch & Technical Foundation
  a) Migration from GoDaddy: new hosting established in Client's name, DNS
     cutover, and a complete 301 redirect map to preserve existing equity.
  b) Technical SEO implementation: title tags and meta descriptions, header
     structure, internal linking, XML sitemap and robots.txt, image compression
     and speed optimization, mobile and Core Web Vitals compliance, SSL, and
     canonical tags.
  c) Schema markup: Organization, Service, LocalBusiness, and FAQ.
  d) Google Business Profile optimization: categories, services, service areas,
     description, photos, and seeded Q&A, linked to the new site.
  e) Measurement configured in Client-owned accounts: Google Analytics 4, Google
     Search Console, and form and call tracking.

  Deliverables: live website, fully migrated and instrumented.

4. REPORTING AND PERFORMANCE EXPECTATIONS

Provider will deliver a written report monthly, plus one strategy call.

Client acknowledges that search performance compounds over time and that no
specific ranking, traffic volume, or lead volume is guaranteed. Expected
progression:

  Days 30-60    Leading indicators: pages indexed, impressions in Search
                Console, site speed, resolution of crawl errors.
  Days 60-120   Movement across the approximately 40 tracked search terms and
                increased presence across the 20 tracked AI prompts.
  Month 4+      Inbound form fills and calls from decision makers, tracked and
                attributed.


5. EXCLUSIONS

The following are expressly NOT included in this Scope of Work:

  a) LinkedIn outreach, list building, connection campaigns, and messaging
     sequences (available as a separate service at $850 per month; $1,500 per
     month for both services combined).
  b) Social media content production and posting across YouTube, Instagram, X,
     and Facebook, which remains with Client's existing provider.
  c) Paid advertising management and paid advertising spend of any kind.
  d) Cold email infrastructure and pay-per-lead services (separately quoted at a
     $990 one-time setup and $125 per qualified lead).
  e) Third-party platform costs. Hosting, domain renewal, and SEO tooling are
     billed directly to Client's own accounts at cost, without markup, and are
     estimated at $30-60 per month in total.
  f) Applicant tracking systems, sourcing tools, and other candidate-side
     systems, which remain unchanged.


6. TERMS

6.1 Fee and Billing. $850.00 per month, with no setup fee. The first payment is
due on the commencement date, and monthly thereafter on the same date of each
month.

6.2 Term and Termination. This engagement is month-to-month. Either party may
terminate with thirty (30) days' written notice. No penalty or early termination
fee applies, and no amounts are owed beyond the final month.

6.3 Ownership. Client owns all work product. The domain, hosting account, Google
Analytics, Google Search Console, Google Business Profile, and all website files
and content are registered in Client's name from the commencement date. Upon
termination, all assets remain with Client and the site remains operational.

6.4 Client Responsibilities. Client will provide access to the existing website
and domain registrar and to the Google Business Profile, and will make
approximately one hour of Client time available during month one for approval of
the messaging document, review of the staging site, and confirmation of case
study details.

6.5 Revisions. One full revision round is included on every page during the
build. Unlimited minor edits (copy changes, images, contact details, new service
listings) are included for the duration of the engagement, with a 48-hour
turnaround. A second full redesign is outside this Scope of Work and would be
quoted separately.

6.6 Point of Contact. Kimball Mueller is Client's direct point of contact,
providing one strategy call per month and a written update weekly. Fulfillment
is supported by the Disruptors Media team.

6.7 Commencement. Work begins upon Client's authorization and delivery of the
access described in Section 6.4. There is no onboarding period and no setup
invoice.
