What this does, and what it doesn't

We're not going to promise a number of new customers. Whether a company adopts a technology depends on your science, their trials and their timing. That part is yours, and we're not replacing it.

We're also not going to tell you SEO is fast. A site at 30 to 50 visitors a month takes months to grow, which is why outreach carries the first quarter. And we're not proposing short-form video or paid ads. Neither is how a seed or fertilizer company chooses a technology partner.

What changes is who hears from you: named people at the accounts you chose, every week, whether or not a referral shows up that month. We'll report on replies and meetings from target accounts and on inquiries from the site. We won't report on followers or open rates.
