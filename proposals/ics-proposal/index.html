01

You take work nationwide. Everything about your web presence says Tuscaloosa.

You told us plainly that geography is not a limit — if it is metal, you will coat it, and you will take it from anywhere. Your site names exactly one place: 4711 Fosters Industrial Lane. Google has no reason to show ICS to a fabricator in Ohio, and the AI answer engines have nothing of yours to cite. You are effectively invisible in every market except the one you can already drive to.

02

Your strongest proof is sitting as plain text on an About page.

Mercedes. ZF. Metalsa. Bolzoni Auramo. Hyster-Yale. AT&T. Comcast. Alabama Power. That is a list most coaters in the country cannot match, and it is a paragraph nobody scrolls to. There are no project photos, no case studies, no finished-part gallery and no capacity numbers anywhere a buyer looks first. A purchasing manager comparing three shops has no way to tell which one is serious.

03

Nothing has been published in almost three years.

Your blog ran ten posts through 2023 and stopped on December 5, 2023. Google rewards recent and relevant material, and ChatGPT and Gemini will not cite a site that has gone quiet. In the meantime, referrals and a few local banners are carrying the entire load of finding new work.

Audited September 1, 2026 at ics-team1.com. You called the website terrible. It is fairer to say it is a Weebly brochure doing brochure work — it is not broken, it is just not reaching. Your powder coating page runs roughly 300 words, names one city, and offers no route in for anyone who has not already heard your name. That is a reach problem, not a design problem, and the fix is different.

Two buyers. They do not find you the same way, and one of them will never go looking.

Audience one

The buyer already searching

A purchasing manager or plant engineer at a 25 to 50 person manufacturer whose current coater just missed a deadline, raised prices or lost a line. They are looking today. They open Google or ChatGPT and type a part type, a finish and a state.

Reached through search and AI answersThis buyer is the cheapest one you will ever win, because they arrive already convinced they need the service. But you are either on that page or you are not in the conversation, and today you are not on that page outside Alabama.

Fabricators, machine shops and OEM suppliers coating in-house or using someone merely adequate. They are not unhappy enough to search. Nothing you publish will reach them, because they will never type the query.

What this does not do

We are not going to tell you that a rebuilt website brings in six accounts. It does not. A good site converts the buyer who was already going to find you, and it takes roughly six to nine months of published pages and articles before search starts sending you people who never would have found you at all. Anyone promising you rankings by Christmas is selling something.

What moves on a shorter clock is outreach. Cold email and LinkedIn work in weeks, not quarters — they are the only levers on this list that can produce a conversation inside your first sixty days. That is the real trade between the tiers, and it is a trade about timing, not about quality.

So the honest sequence is this. Start the slow work now, because it only gets slower the longer it waits. Add the fast work when you want the third shift on a date rather than eventually. If that date is inside twelve months, Movement-Maker is the tier that gets you there and we would rather say so plainly than let you find out in month nine.

How to judge it: against one account of yours, not against agency rates. A single steady account at the bottom of your own range — $2,000 a week, 45 weeks — is $90,000 a year. That covers the top tier for a full year more than twice over. Third-party software and any advertising spend are billed directly to ICS at cost, in your name, with no markup. Not included: ad spend, third-party software and list data, sending domains and inbox costs, travel, legal fees.
