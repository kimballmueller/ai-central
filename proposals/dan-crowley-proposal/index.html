What this won't do

It won't turn you into an influencer, and it won't go viral in the first month. We won't post anything you haven't approved, we won't use a client's name or numbers you'd want kept private, and we'll stick to what you can prove with the math, because that's what sets you apart from everyone else in the feed.

What it will do is put a trustworthy voice next to the DSCR ads. When the person in trouble finally looks for help, your face is the one they already know.

How to judge it: compare it with your placement fees, not with agency rates. The year costs $18,000. You know your fees better than we do. If a single conversation that starts with a clip turns into one placed loan, the year is probably paid for. Not included: paid promotion, third-party software, travel.
