What the benchmarks actually mean

For planning, we will use a conservative 25–35% connection acceptance rate, 8–15% reply rate among accepted connections, and a 20–30% close rate on qualified calls. Those are operating assumptions, not promises. Dripify reports a 43% average acceptance rate and 23%+ reply rate across its users; we are planning below those vendor-reported figures. LinkedIn warns that high invitation volume or suspected automation may lead to restrictions, so relevance and account safety take priority over volume.
