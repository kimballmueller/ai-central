# Scope of Work — Ken Lund Revenue and Content Growth System

This Scope of Work (“SOW”) is between Disruptors Media (“Disruptors Media”) and Kenneth W. Lund / Nosilla Relyt Incorporated (“Client”). It describes a month-to-month engagement focused on building and operating a revenue and content growth system.

## 1. Objective

Disruptors Media will help Client pursue the following growth targets:

- $10,000 in new, attributable collected revenue during a rolling 30-day period within the first 90 days.
- $20,000 in new, attributable collected revenue during a rolling 30-day period within the first 180 days.

These figures are targets used to guide the work and adjust compensation. They are not guarantees of performance.

## 2. Initial strategy

The first 90 days will prioritize the shortest credible path to revenue: acquiring premium mentoring clients, initially focused on qualified owners and senior executives in post-acute healthcare. Once the initial revenue engine is producing results, Disruptors Media will help Client test one scalable offer, which may include a paid writing product, leadership library, peer cohort, team license, or group program.

## 3. Services and deliverables

Disruptors Media will provide:

1. Audience and offer development
   - Define the initial ideal client profile and qualification criteria.
   - Package one primary premium mentoring offer, including positioning, price, proof, and call to action.
   - Recommend the sequence of future writing, cohort, library, or group offers.

2. LinkedIn and website conversion improvements
   - Recommend and draft revisions to Client’s LinkedIn positioning and primary calls to action.
   - Recommend and draft revisions to the key website sections required to support the active offer.
   - Organize available client outcomes and stories into usable case-study or proof language.

3. LinkedIn acquisition campaigns
   - Define prospect-list criteria and build or coordinate campaign prospect lists.
   - Write connection, follow-up, and re-engagement sequences.
   - Configure and manage approved outreach campaigns using Dripify or a comparable platform.
   - Monitor acceptance, replies, qualified conversations, and booked calls and adjust targeting and copy accordingly.
   - Client will personally participate in substantive sales conversations and approve any messages that are presented as personally written by Client.

4. Meetings and campaign management
   - Two mandatory working calls per calendar month: one strategy and pipeline call and one recorded content interview.
   - Additional calls during offset weeks may be scheduled by mutual agreement when reasonably necessary for execution.
   - A twice-monthly campaign scorecard covering prospects added, acceptance rate, reply rate, qualified conversations, calls, proposals, collected revenue, and next actions.

5. Content system
   - Develop interview questions based on Client’s existing writing, experience, and the active campaign.
   - Record guided conversations with Client and identify short-form content segments.
   - Repurpose Client’s existing writing and recorded conversations into campaign-supporting LinkedIn posts and short-form video concepts or edits, subject to the monthly production capacity agreed during planning.
   - Maintain one primary content call to action aligned with the current campaign.

6. Second revenue offer
   - After sufficient early revenue or demand signals exist, define and test one scalable offer such as a paid writing product, peer cohort, leadership library, or team license.
   - This SOW includes offer design, message development, and a limited market test. Full course production, large content-library production, or a custom member platform requires a separate written scope.

## 4. Client responsibilities

Client will:

- Attend the two mandatory monthly calls and provide timely feedback and approvals.
- Provide access to the accounts and materials reasonably required for the work, including LinkedIn, website, Substack or email platform, analytics, and existing case-study information.
- Review and respond to interested prospects in a timely manner and personally conduct mentoring sales calls unless otherwise agreed.
- Continue producing original writing at a mutually realistic cadence.
- Make truthful, supportable claims and obtain any required permissions before a client name, quote, result, or confidential story is used publicly.
- Pay approved third-party software and outside costs directly.

Delays in access, approvals, content, prospect responses, or sales follow-up may affect timing and results.

## 5. Fees and revenue-linked adjustments

The initial management fee is $1,500 per month.

The $1,500 monthly fee remains in effect until attributable collected revenue reaches $10,000 during a rolling 30-day period. Beginning with the next monthly billing cycle after that benchmark is verified, the management fee increases to a minimum of $3,500 per month.

After the $10,000 benchmark is reached, the parties may mutually agree in writing to expand the engagement to $5,000 per month based on performance, workload, and the approved scope. The $5,000 level is not automatic unless confirmed in writing.

When attributable collected revenue reaches $20,000 during a rolling 30-day period, the management fee increases to $8,000 per month beginning with the next monthly billing cycle.

## 6. Definition of attributable collected revenue

“Attributable collected revenue” means cash actually received by Client from a customer or client whose first recorded source was an agreed Disruptors Media campaign, landing page, sequence, content call to action, or tracked referral path created or managed under this SOW.

Attributable collected revenue excludes:

- Revenue from clients who were already paying Client before this engagement.
- Revenue from opportunities already active before this engagement unless both parties identify them in writing as campaign-assisted.
- Uncollected invoices, unsigned proposals, taxes, refunds, credits, and chargebacks.
- Unrelated referrals or sales with no documented connection to the agreed work.

Client will provide reasonable access to the records needed to verify campaign source and collected revenue. The parties will review attribution during the twice-monthly pipeline meetings and resolve disputed attribution in good faith before a fee adjustment is applied.

## 7. Third-party costs

Third-party costs are not included in the management fee. Expected initial costs include Dripify, quoted during the discovery call at approximately $78 per month, and may later include LinkedIn Sales Navigator, email software, hosting, editing tools, or advertising. No new third-party cost will be incurred without Client’s approval. Disruptors Media will not mark up approved software or media costs.

## 8. Items not included

Unless added by written change order, this SOW does not include:

- Paid advertising budgets or media buying.
- A full website redesign or custom software development.
- Full production of a large online course, member portal, or extensive leadership library.
- Daily community management or unlimited social posting.
- In-person filming, travel, events, printing, or physical-product marketing.
- Legal, accounting, compliance, or intellectual-property advice.
- Guaranteed lead, revenue, subscriber, or customer outcomes.

## 9. Ownership and use

Client owns the accounts, final approved Client content, campaign data, and custom assets created specifically for Client after applicable invoices are paid. Disruptors Media retains ownership of its pre-existing processes, templates, general methods, software, and know-how. Client grants Disruptors Media permission to access and use Client-provided materials only as needed to perform this SOW.

## 10. Term and termination

The engagement is month to month. Either party may end the engagement by providing 30 days’ written notice. Fees and approved expenses incurred through the effective termination date remain due. On termination, Disruptors Media will provide Client with reasonable access to Client-owned accounts and completed deliverables.

## 11. Performance standard

Disruptors Media will perform the services in a professional and commercially reasonable manner. Marketing and sales results depend on factors outside Disruptors Media’s control, including market response, Client participation, offer quality, pricing, platform rules, sales execution, and economic conditions. The revenue targets and planning benchmarks in this SOW are goals rather than warranties or guarantees.
