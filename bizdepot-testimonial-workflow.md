# BizDepot Seller Testimonial — Repeatable Workflow

The system every future deal plugs into. Amarillo is the first run, not a one-off.

**The pipeline:** Deal closes → BizDepot sends the deal brief → Joe introduces Kimball → date scheduled → seller gets the prep page → logistics confirmed → Kimball shoots solo → edit → BizDepot approval → flagship film + cutdown matrix.

---

## Step 1 — BizDepot sends the deal brief

Before the seller hears from me, Joe/BizDepot sends this. Nothing gets figured out with the camera running.

**Seller**
- Full name + preferred name
- Cell, email
- Business name / DBA
- Exact filming address
- What the business does
- Years owned/operated · team size

**Deal**
- Closing date
- Sale price — approved for public discussion? Y/N
- Buyer — nameable? Y/N
- Timeline from first BizDepot call to closing
- BizDepot people the seller actually worked with (by name)
- Anything under NDA or otherwise off-limits
- Who at BizDepot approves the finished video

**Scheduling**
- 2–3 possible filming dates
- Confirmation the seller can give ~4 uninterrupted hours

## Step 2 — Joe makes the introduction

Group text: Joe + seller + Kimball.

> "John, introducing you to Kimball. He handles our documentary/testimonial filming for BizDepot and will be taking care of everything for the shoot. Kimball, I'll let you two coordinate directly from here."

That's all Joe has to do. From that text forward, Kimball owns production communication — and shows up with BizDepot's authority without an advisor on site.

## Step 3 — Seller gets the prep page

One link: `bizdepot_seller_prep.html`. Six things, four-hour timeline, don't-prepare-a-speech. Fill in date, address and phone before sending.

## Step 4 — Confirmation call (optional)

10–15 minutes, a few days out. Logistics only — location, people, equipment on site. Not a pre-interview. Skip it if everything's already locked.

## Step 5 — Shoot day

1. **Meet the seller.** Don't unpack cameras. Build rapport first.
2. **Walk the property.** They show me the business; I'm learning the story and scouting shots at the same time.
3. **Sign the release.** Joe isn't there — this is on me.
4. **Lock the interview room.** Cameras, lighting, sound. ~45 min.
5. **The interview.** Seven acts, 60–90 min. Story arc, not corporate Q&A.
6. **Cinematic B-roll.** Seller working, walking, with crew and equipment; trucks, yard, signage, office, hands, details.
7. **Historical material.** Old photos and objects — including the seller physically going through them. Usually the strongest emotional footage of the day.
8. **Pickups.** Missing sound bites, exteriors, drone if applicable.
9. **Wrap.** Thank them, tell them what happens next.

## Step 6 — Post

Flagship film + 8–12 vertical cutdowns + transcript and schema kit → BizDepot review → final assets.
