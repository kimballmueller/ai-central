Color

The palette is a blue → indigo → violet ramp on near-black. Blue is dominant, violet is the bridge, pink is a kiss of accent — never a fill. Click any swatch to copy the hex.

Gradients

Four gradients do all the work. All run at 135°. Don't invent new ones — pick the one whose job matches.

Primary — buttons & CTAs
linear-gradient(135deg, #2563eb 0%, #4f46e5 60%, #8b5cf6 100%)
Every primary action. Note the 60% stop — it's not an even three-way split.
Primary Hover / Logo Mark
linear-gradient(135deg, #3b82f6 0%, #6366f1 60%, #a78bfa 100%)
The whole primary gradient lifted one step. Also the logo mark fill.
Gradient Text
linear-gradient(135deg, #93c5fd 0%, #c4b5fd 100%)
Clipped to text for eyebrows and section labels only. Never body copy.
Mesh Background
3 radial blobs, blur(90px): blue .70 · indigo .45 · pink .32 — on #0a0a1a
The signature backdrop. Blue biggest and top-left, pink smallest and bottom-right.

Typography

One family: the system UI stack, with Inter as the named fallback. If a design tool needs a single font file, use Inter — it's the closest match and is free on Google Fonts. Headings tighten (−0.02em), uppercase labels open up (+0.08em). That contrast is the typographic identity.

Logo Mark

A rounded square holding a single white K. Corner radius is 22% of the square — it scales with the mark, so don't hard-code a pixel value.

Voice

Plain-spoken, never dumbed down. The tagline is the tone contract: “AI so simple your mom gets it.” Explain the hard thing in the words a roofing owner already uses.

Proof over promise. Real dashboards, real call logs, real numbers from real businesses. Screenshots beat adjectives every time.

Two audiences, one story. Owners who want it built for them, operators who want to build it themselves. Every piece serves both and ends with the fork.

Do

  • Dark backgrounds. This brand lives at night.
  • Let the mesh gradient breathe — big, soft, out of focus.
  • Blue leads. Violet supports. Pink whispers.
  • Generous corner radii. Nothing sharp.

Don't

  • Put the logo on white without testing — the glow disappears.
  • Use pink as a fill, a button, or a text color.
  • Add a second typeface. One family, many weights.
  • Stretch, recolor, or outline the K mark.
  • Mix in Kimball Travels branding. Different brand.
