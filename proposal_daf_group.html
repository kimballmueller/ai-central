You closed the clients you have now off nothing but reputation, so I’d expect better than 25%. That’s the whole model.

Three reasons this channel works for a design firm specifically. First, your buyer is on LinkedIn — GCs, developers and multifamily investors keep active profiles because that’s where their own deal flow comes from; a homeowner isn’t on there, and a homeowner isn’t your client. Second, you have no service area. You said it yourself: “it doesn’t matter on location, we can work almost everywhere.” Most firms burn out a local list in ninety days. Yours is national and effectively doesn’t run out. Third, the first message isn’t a pitch — it’s a design perspective on the kind of project they’re already building. It costs them nothing to reply.

The sequencing matters, and it’s built around your cash situation

The first sixty days point at general contractors and builders, because they have projects in hand right now and a shorter path to a signed scope — that’s the fastest route to money in the door. Once that’s producing, the campaign rotates onto multifamily developers and investors, which is the longer cycle but the bigger project. We don’t start with the slow one while you’re waiting on five clients to pay you.
