Selling into companies with hundreds or thousands of employees means your email sometimes lands in front of an executive assistant rather than the VP, and assistants are paid to delete exactly this. Your buyer is unusually easy to name — there is one person at each of these companies who owns merit, and they own it personally. And your subject line isn't a product; it's the spreadsheet they are going to open again in January and hate again in January. We are not sending “HR tech solution.” We're sending them their own worst month. If we are ninety days in and the reply rate is under 2%, I will tell you before you have to ask me, and we move that budget to whatever is working instead.

The campaign changes shape three times, on your calendar, not ours. Your business has one buying season a year. Nothing here pretends otherwise.
